## Brand & Style

This design system targets high-net-worth property investors, architectural connoisseurs, and boutique real estate clientele seeking understated exclusivity. The aesthetic rejects decorative tech cliches—eliminating vibrant saturated gradient meshes, neon accent buttons, and playful novelty elements—in favor of quiet luxury, generous negative space, and disciplined spatial geometry. 

The visual style merges Swiss editorial discipline with warm architectural minimalism:
- **Atmospheric Clarity:** Airy, luminous compositions driven by gallery-like whitespace, precision alignments, and pristine structural borders.
- **Architectural Primacy:** Full bleed and framed architectural renders and photography remain the emotional anchor, treated like fine art exhibits with balanced negative space.
- **Understated Command:** Deep carbon-black interactive elements anchor the canvas, delivering decisive tactile affordances without shouting.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in monochromatic discipline, drawing warmth from subtle paper-tone canvas whites and deep structural blacks. 

- **Primary (`#111111`):** Deep carbon obsidian. Applied to primary call-to-action buttons, key headline typography, prominent interactive markers, and definitive micro-accents.
- **Secondary (`#71717A`):** Neutral zinc slate. Calibrated for secondary metadata, property specifications (bed/bath/sqft), passive icons, and breadcrumb navigation.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`):** Subdued botanical emerald. Reserved strictly for subtle financial yields, positive capitalization returns, and high-performance metrics (+10.8% ROI).
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FBFBFB` & `#FFFFFF`):** Architectural off-white base surfaces layered against pure crisp white component containers and floating card decks.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E5E7EB` & `#F3F4F6`):** Razor-thin structural lines providing spatial organization without visual clutter.
- **Cartographic Grayscale:** Map tiles run entirely desaturated with light gray arterial roads (`#E5E7EB`), soft water surfaces (`#F3F4F6`), and crisp obsidian map pins.

## Typography

The typographical scale is built on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its geometric precision, modern proportions, and sculpted editorial poise. 

- **Display & Headings:** Characterized by tight negative tracking (`-0.025em` to `-0.03em`), grounding large headlines with confidence and structural gravity.
- **Section Kickers & Eyebrows:** Styled in uppercase with wide letter spacing (`0.08em`), delivering an editorial gallery cadence before section headings.
- **Data & Numeric Displays:** High-contrast statistics (`$1B+`, `8–12%`) leverage bold font weights paired with stacked muted labels in `label-sm` to maintain clarity.
- **Editorial Legibility:** Body copy maintains a strict 1.6x line-height ratio, set in neutral slate tones to preserve a soft reading rhythm against stark off-white backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column responsive grid with a maximum content boundary of `1280px`, surrounded by generous canvas breathing room.

- **Vertical Cadence:** Hero sections, gallery blocks, and feature sections employ expansive vertical pads (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) to echo high-end architectural openness.
- **Component Padding:** Inner card groupings and floating filter consoles use systematic 8px increments (`space-md` to `space-xl`).
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, and `3rem` canvas margins. Floating search consoles and metric stat bars span centered across 8 to 10 columns.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, and `2rem` margins. Multicolumn listing cards transition to a 2-column format.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, and `1.25rem` margins. The floating pill search bar stacks vertical segmented fields into an accordion or fixed bottom-sheet modal.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses ambient diffusion rather than heavy dropshadows, evoking natural daylight falling across modern architectural surfaces.

- **Flat Baseline Surfaces:** Main canvas screens sit at z-index zero on `#FBFBFB` with crisp `#E5E7EB` borders defining secondary quadrants.
- **Level 1 (Property Cards & Feature Panels):** Crisp pure white backgrounds (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a 1px border (`#F3F4F6`), finished with a faint daylight haze: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Filter Bars & Map Flyouts):** The signature floating pill search bar and active map property cards utilize stacked diffuse shadows: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogues & Context Menus):** High-float surfaces utilize a deep atmospheric drop: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.10)`.
- **Backdrop Frost:** Floating elements over photographs or grayscale map layers apply an ultra-subtle translucent white tint (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`) paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.

## Shapes

The design system pairs rounded rectangles for content surfaces with pill geometries for high-touch interactive elements.

- **Pill Geometry (`rounded-full` / `9999px`):** Reserved for primary navigation pills, the floating search console container, interactive tags, category pills, and standard primary buttons.
- **Architectural Curvature (`rounded-xl` to `rounded-2xl` / `16px` to `20px`):** Applied to property cards, photography frames, and analytical preview modules, striking a balance between soft modernism and structural precision.
- **Micro-Inputs (`rounded-lg` / `8px` to `12px`):** Used inside property card nested actions, input field dropdowns, and photo gallery thumbnail previews.

## Components

### Floating Search Pill Console
- **Structure:** Centered horizontal floating capsule container (`background: #FFFFFF`, `border: 1px solid #E5E7EB`, `border-radius: 9999px`, elevation Level 2).
- **Segments:** Split into inline segments (*Location*, *Property Type*, *Budget*) separated by fine vertical dividers (`#E5E7EB`, height 60%).
- **Interactive State:** Hovering over a segment activates a soft tint (`#F9FAFB`). A deep carbon circular search button (`#111111`, white magnifying glass icon) anchors the far right.

### Buttons & Interactive Affordances
- **Primary Button:** Carbon obsidian background (`#111111`), crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`), `font-weight: 500`, fully rounded pill shape (`rounded-full`), padded `12px 24px`. Micro-hover states lift by 1px with subtle opacity reduction (`#262626`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, structural border (`1px solid #E5E7EB`), text `#111111`. Hover fills with `#F4F4F5`.
- **Icon / Arrow Badges:** Compact circular triggers (`32px` to `40px`) in obsidian black with directional arrows (`→` or `↗`) for card links and property tours.

### Property Listing Cards
- **Container:** Pure white card body (`#FFFFFF`), `16px` border-radius, fine hairline border (`1px solid #F3F4F6`), and elevation Level 1.
- **Image Header:** Edge-to-edge or framed photography with `12px` interior radius, `4:3` aspect ratio, featuring an absolute-positioned top tag (`New`, `Exclusive`, or `Featured`) in white pill styling.
- **Specs Strip:** Typography row showing inline icons for bedrooms, baths, and square footage in neutral zinc (`#71717A`).
- **Financial Footer:** Prominent price display in `headline-sm` (`#111111`) accompanied by an inline tertiary return indicator (`+10.8%` in `#10B981`) paired with an instant primary CTA button (`Invest Now` / `View Details`).

### Map Micro-Pins & Popovers
- **Map Pins:** Minimal obsidian black circular discs (`#111111`) containing property counts or prices in crisp white typography. Hovering expands the pin to display a thumbnail preview badge.
- **Map Focus Card:** A floating mini-card anchored to the active pin featuring a compact `16:9` preview, title, specs, and direct booking CTA.

### Metric Stat Band
- **Structure:** Clean horizontal multi-column band positioned directly beneath hero sections.
- **Styling:** Large numerical metrics (`$1B+`, `150K+`, `8–12%`) rendered in bold carbon, paired with uppercase muted secondary descriptive labels (`TOTAL INVESTED`, `PROPERTY LISTED`).
